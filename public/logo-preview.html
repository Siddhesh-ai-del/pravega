USAGE NOTES

Primary logo — use on concrete/white backgrounds. The hazard-orange period is the single accent.
Dark variant — use on black/dark backgrounds. Concrete text, orange accent.
Mark only — use for favicon, app icon, avatar, social media profile. Works down to 16×16.

Typography: Fira Sans Condensed 900 (display) + JetBrains Mono 700 (micro-label).
Colors: Ink #0A0A0A · Concrete #D6D3CC · Paper #F5F3ED · Spot #FF4A00 · Muted #6B6560
Rules: No rounded corners. No gradients. No shadows. Hard edges only. The orange accent appears exactly once per logo instance.
Minimum size: 80px wide for full logo, 16px for mark only.
Clear space: 1× the height of the "P" in PRAVEGA on all sides.
